## What Is ECSI?

ECSI (Educational Computer Systems, Inc.) is a specialized loan servicer that primarily handles **Federal Perkins Loans** and other institutional loans on behalf of colleges and universities. Unlike other servicers on this list, ECSI does not service Direct Loans — it services Perkins Loans that are owned by your school.

This is an important distinction: Perkins Loans have **different rules, eligibility, and cancellation programs** than Direct Loans.

## Perkins Loans vs. Direct Loans: Key Differences

| Feature | Perkins Loans | Direct Loans |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Your school | Department of Education |
| Servicer | ECSI (on behalf of your school) | MOHELA, Nelnet, Aidvantage, etc. |
| SAVE/IBR/PAYE | Not eligible | Eligible |
| PSLF | Not eligible directly | Eligible |
| Perkins Cancellation | **Yes — up to 100%** | No |

## Perkins Loan Cancellation Programs

The biggest opportunity for ECSI borrowers is Perkins Loan **cancellation** — which can eliminate 100% of your Perkins balance if you work in qualifying public service roles.

Cancellation percentages grow over years of qualifying service:
- Years 1–2: 15% per year
- Years 3–4: 20% per year
- Year 5: 30%
- **Total: 100% after 5 years**

**Qualifying occupations include:**
- Teachers at low-income schools or in shortage subjects (math, science, special ed)
- Nurses and medical technicians
- Law enforcement officers and firefighters
- Social workers, speech pathologists, and family service workers
- Early childhood educators
- Librarians at Title I schools

Contact your school's financial aid office or ECSI to start the cancellation process.

## What 2026 Policy Changes Mean for ECSI Borrowers

**SAVE, IBR, PAYE, and PSLF do not directly apply to Perkins Loans.** The 2026 court injunction and plan changes affect Direct Loans only.

However, if you also have Direct Loans with another servicer, those loans are subject to the SAVE injunction.

**To access PSLF with Perkins Loans:** You can consolidate your Perkins Loans into a Direct Consolidation Loan — but this makes them ineligible for Perkins Cancellation. You generally should not consolidate Perkins Loans if you qualify for Perkins Cancellation, as cancellation is typically more generous.

## Key Actions for ECSI Borrowers

1. **Determine if you qualify for Perkins Loan cancellation** — this is usually the best option for public service workers.
2. **If you don't qualify for Perkins cancellation:** You may want to consolidate into a Direct Loan and pursue PSLF or IDR — but run the math first.
3. **Contact your school's financial aid office** — Perkins cancellation applications go through your school, not directly through ECSI.
4. **Don't miss payments:** Unlike some other loan programs, Perkins Loans don't have all the same administrative forbearance protections as Direct Loans.
