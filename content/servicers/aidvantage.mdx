## What Is Aidvantage?

Aidvantage is a federal student loan servicer operated by Maximus. In December 2021, Navient transferred its entire federal loan portfolio — about 5.6 million borrowers — to Aidvantage. If your loans were previously with Navient, they are now with Aidvantage.

Aidvantage services **Direct Loans** and **Federal Family Education Loan (FFEL) Program** loans that have been consolidated into Direct Loans.

## What 2026 Policy Changes Mean for Aidvantage Borrowers

**If you are enrolled in the SAVE Plan:** Your payments are currently paused due to a federal court injunction. You are in administrative forbearance. These paused months **do not count** toward IDR forgiveness or PSLF. Log in to aidvantage.com to confirm your current plan status.

**If you want to switch from SAVE:** You can request enrollment in IBR (Income-Based Repayment) through your Aidvantage account or at studentaid.gov. IBR remains fully operational and new enrollment is open.

**If you are pursuing PSLF:** PSLF processing is handled by MOHELA, not Aidvantage. You will need to submit an Employer Certification Form through studentaid.gov. Contact MOHELA directly for payment count questions.

**FFEL borrowers:** If your loans are FFEL (not Direct Loans), they do not qualify for SAVE, PAYE, or PSLF. You may need to consolidate into a Direct Consolidation Loan first — which resets your forgiveness clock.

## Key Actions for Aidvantage Borrowers

1. **Log in to aidvantage.com** and verify your current repayment plan.
2. **If on SAVE:** Decide whether to switch to IBR now or wait for the injunction to resolve. Payments during the SAVE forbearance do not count toward forgiveness.
3. **If pursuing PSLF:** Submit your Employer Certification Form annually and after any job change. Check your qualifying payment count at studentaid.gov.
4. **If your loans are FFEL:** Consider a Direct Consolidation Loan, but understand that consolidation resets any forgiveness progress.

## Common Aidvantage Problems and Solutions

**"My payment amount doesn't seem right."** Your IDR payment recalculates annually based on your most recent tax return. If your income changed significantly, you can request an early recertification through Aidvantage.

**"I can't log in to Aidvantage."** Aidvantage inherited Navient's accounts. If you haven't set up your Aidvantage account yet, visit aidvantage.com and use your SSN to create a new login — your loan data should be pre-populated.

**"I got a notice about transferring to a new servicer."** The Department of Education has been moving loans between servicers. Always update your contact information at studentaid.gov so you receive transfer notices.
